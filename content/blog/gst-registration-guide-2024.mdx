## What is GST Registration?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) registration is the process by which a business obtains a unique Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) from the government. This 15-digit number is mandatory for businesses meeting certain turnover thresholds and allows them to legally collect GST from customers and claim Input Tax Credit (ITC).

## Who Must Register for GST?

GST registration is mandatory for:

- Businesses with annual aggregate turnover exceeding **₹40 lakhs** (goods) or **₹20 lakhs** (services)
- Businesses supplying goods inter-state (regardless of turnover)
- E-commerce operators and sellers on e-commerce platforms
- Casual taxable persons and non-resident taxable persons
- Businesses required to pay tax under reverse charge mechanism

## Documents Required

1. PAN Card of the business/applicant
2. Aadhar Card of the proprietor/partners/directors
3. Photograph of the applicant
4. Proof of business premises (rent agreement/utility bill)
5. Bank account statement or cancelled cheque
6. Partnership deed / Certificate of Incorporation (for entities)

## Step-by-Step GST Registration Process

### Step 1: Visit the GST Portal
Go to **gstin.gov.in** and click on "New Registration."

### Step 2: Fill Part A of the Application
Enter your PAN, mobile number, and email address. An OTP will be sent to verify.

### Step 3: Fill Part B with Business Details
Enter business name, address, business constitution, nature of goods/services, and bank details.

### Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload the required documents in the prescribed format.

### Step 5: Submit and Receive ARN
After submission, you receive an Application Reference Number (ARN).

### Step 6: Receive GSTIN
Within 3-7 working days, you receive your GSTIN and GST Registration Certificate.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Wrong business category**: Selecting the wrong business category can cause rejection
- **Incorrect address**: Address mismatch with utility bills causes delays
- **Not mentioning all places of business**: Multiple branches must be registered
- **PAN mismatch**: Business PAN must match exactly with Income Tax records
