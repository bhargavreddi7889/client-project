## Introduction

Filing your Income Tax Return (ITR) correctly can save you thousands in taxes and prevent future notices from the Income Tax Department. Here are 10 essential tips every taxpayer should know before filing their ITR for FY 2024-25.

## 1. Choose the Right ITR Form

Using the wrong form can invalidate your return. Here's a quick guide:
- **ITR-1 (Sahaj)**: Salaried individuals with income up to ₹50 lakhs
- **ITR-2**: Capital gains, multiple properties, foreign assets
- **ITR-3**: Business/profession income
- **ITR-4 (Sugam)**: Presumptive taxation under Section 44AD/44ADA

## 2. Reconcile Form 26AS and AIS

Always check your Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Form 26AS before filing. Any mismatch between your income and these documents will trigger a notice from the IT department.

## 3. Claim All Eligible Deductions

Don't miss these common deductions:
- **Section 80C**: Up to ₹1.5 lakhs (PPF, ELSS, LIC, home loan principal)
- **Section 80D**: Health insurance premiums (up to ₹25,000; ₹50,000 for senior citizens)
- **Section 80G**: Charitable donations
- **HRA Exemption**: If you pay rent and receive HRA
- **Home Loan Interest** (Section 24b): Up to ₹2 lakhs

## 4. Old Tax Regime vs New Tax Regime

For FY 2024-25, the new tax regime is the default. Calculate your tax liability under both regimes and opt for whichever results in lower tax. Salaried individuals with significant deductions often benefit from the old regime.

## 5. Don't Forget Capital Gains

Report all capital gains:
- **STCG on equity**: 20% (FY 2024-25 onwards)
- **LTCG on equity**: 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakhs
- **Debt fund gains**: Taxed at slab rate (no indexation from FY 2023-24)

## 6. Report All Income Sources

Many taxpayers miss reporting:
- Interest from savings accounts (above ₹10,000)
- Fixed deposit interest
- Freelance income
- Rental income from property

Failure to report income is treated as tax evasion.

## 7. Pay Advance Tax If Applicable

If your tax liability exceeds ₹10,000 (after TDS), you must pay advance tax. Missing advance tax deadlines attracts interest under Section 234B and 234C.

## 8. Verify Your Return

After filing, e-verify your ITR within 30 days. You can verify via:
- Aadhar-based OTP
- Net banking
- Bank ATM
- Sending signed ITR-V to CPC Bengaluru

## 9. File Before the Due Date

Due dates for FY 2024-25:
- **Individuals (non-audit)**: July 31, 2025
- **Audit cases**: October 31, 2025
- **Belated return**: December 31, 2025

Late filing fee: ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if income ≤ ₹5 lakhs)

## 10. Keep Records for 6 Years

Maintain all supporting documents for 6 years — investment proofs, rent receipts, Form 16, bank statements, and capital gains statements. The IT department can reopen assessments for up to 6 years.
